# Urdu voice sample

`fleurs_ur_pk_test_0.ogg` is Google FLEURS, Urdu/Pakistan (`ur_pk`), test row 0,
record ID 1806. Source: [Google FLEURS dataset and dataset card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/google/fleurs).
Licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
Credit: Google FLEURS authors and speech contributors. The original WAV was
transcoded to OGG/Opus; speech content was not edited. The adjacent JSON records
the reference transcript and the redistributed audio's SHA-256.

This is general Urdu read speech. It is **not** a COD customer reply, a merchant
record, or evidence of recognition accuracy across Pakistani customers. It is
included to make the real audio-to-transcript smoke test reproducible. A judge
can upload or record a different short Urdu reply in the dashboard.
